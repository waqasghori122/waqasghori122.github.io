About the Basic Calculator

A simple online calculator for everyday sums. Use the keypad to add (+), subtract (-), multiply (x) and divide (/), calculate percentages and use brackets. Press = or Calculate to see the answer. You can also type with your keyboard.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I multiply and divide?

Press the x key to multiply and the divide key to divide. The calculator follows the normal order of operations, so multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction.

How does the percent key work?

The % key divides the number before it by 100. For example, 50% becomes 0.5.
