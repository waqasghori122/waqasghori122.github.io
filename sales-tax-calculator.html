About the Sales Tax Calculator

Add sales tax, VAT or GST to a price, or remove it from a total. Adding tax: price x rate / 100. Removing tax from a price that includes it: price - price / (1 + rate/100). Results are estimates and may differ from official or bank figures.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How is the sales tax calculator calculated?
