About the Age Calculator

This age calculator finds the exact time between your date of birth and any other date. Pick day, month and year from the lists, press Calculate and see your age in years, months and days. It also shows total days and weeks lived and how many days remain until your next birthday.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?

Years, months and days are counted from the birth date to the chosen date, with month lengths and leap years taken into account.

Can I find age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Age on date" fields to any date.
