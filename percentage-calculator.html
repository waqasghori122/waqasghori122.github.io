About the Percentage Calculator

This tool answers three common questions at once: what is A percent of B, A is what percent of B, and what is the percentage change from A to B. Useful for discounts, marks, profit and price changes.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a discount?

Use the first result. Enter the discount percent as A and the price as B to get the discount amount.

How is percentage change found?

Subtract A from B, divide by A, then multiply by 100.
