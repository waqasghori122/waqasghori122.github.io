About the Student Loan Calculator

Estimate student loan payments and see the effect of paying extra each month. Payment = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1). With an extra payment, the balance is reduced month by month until it reaches zero. Results are estimates and may differ from official or bank figures.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.
