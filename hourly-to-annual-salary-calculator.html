About the Hourly to Annual Salary Calculator

Convert hourly pay to annual, monthly and weekly salary. Annual pay = hourly pay x hours per week x weeks per year. Results are estimates and may differ from official or bank figures.

How is the hourly to annual salary calculator calculated?
