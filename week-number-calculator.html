About the Week Number Calculator

Find the ISO week number and day of the year for any date. ISO 8601: weeks start on Monday and week 1 is the week with the first Thursday of the year. Results are estimates and may differ from official or bank figures.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How is the week number calculator calculated?

ISO 8601: weeks start on Monday and week 1 is the week with the first Thursday of the year.

Is the result exact?

It is a close estimate based on the values you enter. For important financial, health or legal decisions, check with a qualified professional.
