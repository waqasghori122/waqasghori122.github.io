About the BMR Calculator

Find how many calories your body burns at rest each day. BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age + 5 (men) or - 161 (women). Results are estimates and may differ from official or bank figures.

How to use

  1. Enter your values or choose a file.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Copy, print or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How is the bmr calculator calculated?

BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age + 5 (men) or - 161 (women).

Is the result exact?

It is a close estimate based on the values you enter. For important financial, health or legal decisions, check with a qualified professional.
